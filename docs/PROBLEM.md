# VA Time Tracker

## Who
One virtual assistant (VA): the project owner, tracking their own work through a personal account.

## The struggle
The VA needs a personal record of when they start and finish work each day. They currently write the times on paper, find manual tracking time-consuming, and sometimes lose the paper.

## Today's workaround
Writing start and end times on paper every workday. The paper can be lost, leaving the VA without a dependable record.

## The MVP
A personal time tracker where the VA signs in, records the work date and start and end times, and sees their own saved records.

## How we'll know it worked
In a three-minute demo, the VA signs in, starts and ends a Session, and sees a saved Time record with the date and both times. The VA will try the working version before Session 3.

## Out of scope
- Client details, payroll, or reports.
- Work-type labels.
- Collecting a name just to personalize the greeting; use a neutral “Hello, VA” instead.
- Multiple user roles, teams, or shared Time records.

## Glossary
- **VA**: Virtual assistant; here, the one person using the app to track their own work.
- **Time record**: A work date with the time the VA started and finished work.
- **Session**: One period of work between starting and ending the timer.
- **Owner**: The signed-in VA account allowed to read and change its own Time records.
