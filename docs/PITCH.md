# VA Time Tracker Pitch

## The person

At the end of a workday, I need to remember when I started and when I finished my virtual assistant work. Right now, I write those times on paper. I have lost that paper before, and keeping track this way takes time. This is my own experience as a VA; I have not yet tested whether other VAs have the same problem.

## Today

I track my time by hand every day. The record is only as useful as the piece of paper it is written on. If I lose it, I may lose my personal record too. I want a simple way to keep those times together.

## Our fix

I’m making a personal time tracker where I sign in to keep my records private. I’ll start a Session when I begin work, end it when I finish, and see the saved date and both times. I can access my records wherever I log in, but the app will not collect client details.

The aim is to replace a paper note with a clear record I can find again. I’m keeping the first version deliberately small: no reports, payroll, or work categories. That lets me test the central question first: is this start-and-end flow easier for me than writing my times down?

## The demo moment

In the demo, I’ll press “Start” to record the beginning of a work session. Later, I’ll press “End.” Then I’ll show the saved date, start time, and end time in my records. That is the moment when my record is no longer just on a piece of paper I might lose.

The important part is not just pressing the buttons. I’ll check that the date and times appear together afterward, so I can tell which work session the record belongs to.

## How we’ll know

I’ll try it myself before Session 3. I’ll check that I can start and end a session and find its saved date and times afterward. If that works reliably for me, it will be a useful first version. We still need to ask other VAs whether this problem matters to them before claiming it is a broader need.

I’ll also notice whether the flow feels quicker and easier than writing on paper, and whether I can find the record when I need it. If it does not make those everyday steps easier, then the app has not solved my problem yet. The demo is a first check, not proof that every VA needs this.

## Hard questions

**Why build an app instead of using a spreadsheet or phone notes?**  
That is a fair challenge. A spreadsheet or notes app may be enough. I’m trying a focused start-and-end flow because writing times on paper is time-consuming for me and I have lost the paper. I should compare the app with a simple digital record and keep the app only if it is easier to use.

**Have you confirmed that other VAs have this problem?**  
No. The evidence so far is my own experience. Before presenting this as a problem for VAs generally, I should talk with at least one other VA and ask how they track time and whether losing or maintaining records is a real issue for them.

**What if you forget to press “Start” or “End”?**  
The first version depends on me pressing the buttons. It can record the times I enter, but it cannot prove that I remembered to use it. I’ll test whether the simple flow is easy enough to use during a normal workday.
